The Reference

What we’re studying

The reel showcases a brand site for an AI-health company (“MI”). It isn’t “busy” — it’s three or four full-screen scenes, each one a single line of editorial copy over a cinematic portrait, moving on a slow, smooth scroll. That restraint is exactly what reads as luxury.

The Anatomy

Why it feels premium

Eight techniques do the heavy lifting. None of them are exotic — the craft is in the restraint and the pacing.

01

Type over the subject

A large serif headline sits behind and in front of a cut-out model, creating real depth. The single most “2025” move in the reel.

02

Serif + one italic word

“AI for humans.” A display serif with a single italic accent — the fashion-magazine signature.

03

Cinematic colour grade

Moody gradients — plum, peach, warm shadow — instead of flat white. The whole page looks colour-graded like film.

04

One idea per screen

Each scroll is a full viewport with a single sentence and enormous breathing room. Confidence, not clutter.

05

Floating glass labels

Soft, rounded, semi-transparent pills drift around the subject — texture and life without noise.

06

Slow, smooth scroll

Inertia scrolling plus scroll-triggered reveals and parallax. The motion is what a screenshot can never capture.

07

Story, not specs

Copy sells a feeling (“in the dark → understood”), never a feature list. Emotion first, product second.

08

Micro-interactions

Hover states, cursor cues, gentle image scale. Small responses everywhere make it feel alive and expensive.

The Approach · Shopify + Custom Theme

How we build this — without leaving Shopify

This level of motion doesn’t come from an off-the-shelf theme, but it does not require abandoning Shopify. We build a custom theme and layer in two well-proven animation libraries. Your checkout, inventory, and orders stay exactly as they are.

Foundation
A custom Shopify theme (Liquid sections), so every scene is editable from the Shopify admin — no developer needed for copy or image swaps.
Smooth scroll
Lenis — the inertia scrolling you see in the reel. Lightweight, ~a few KB.
Scroll animation
GSAP + ScrollTrigger — the industry standard for pinned scenes, reveals, and parallax. Runs happily inside a Shopify theme.
Type-over-subject
Background-removed PNG cut-outs of the model, layered with CSS. The one asset we’ll need shot or prepped for it.
Glass labels & hovers
CSS backdrop-filter + small JS for the floating pills and micro-interactions.
Performance
WebP/AVIF images, lazy-loading, and a strict prefers-reduced-motion path so it stays fast and accessible.

What Shopify + custom theme gives us

  • Full cinematic homepage — type-over-subject hero, scroll scenes, smooth inertia scroll
  • Editorial brand-story pages (About, Fabric, Journal) with the same motion language
  • Client-editable scenes in the Shopify admin
  • Untouched, secure Shopify checkout, payments, and inventory

Where the honest limits are

  • No full single-page-app page transitions — we use reveals and fades instead of seamless route morphs
  • Heavy WebGL/3D is possible but not where Liquid shines; we’d keep 3D minimal or skip it
  • Product & collection pages stay motion-light on purpose — shopping speed beats spectacle

To Make It Sing

What we’ll need from you

The design direction is ready. To hit the reference’s quality, a few inputs matter more than anything:

Cut-out-ready photography

A few model/family shots on clean, evenly-lit backgrounds so we can layer type behind the subject. This single asset is what makes or breaks the hero.

Brand story & tone

Sign-off on the narrative voice (“night before”, “worlds to dream in”) so the scroll copy carries feeling, not features.

Logo & type

Vector logo, and either approval of our serif pairing or your preferred brand fonts.

Shopify access

A development theme on your store so we build against the real catalog with zero risk to the live site.

We’re not starting from zero
